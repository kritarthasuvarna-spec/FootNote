Privacy Policy — FootNote

Effective date: September 6, 2026

FootNote ("the app") is a Windows utility for attaching short notes to files. This policy describes what data the app does and does not collect.

Data collection

FootNote does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • All notes you create are stored locally on your own device, saved alongside the file they're attached to. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent to any server.
  • The app does not use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting of any kind.
  • The app does not require or create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information.
  • The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

In-app purchases

FootNote offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Unlock Full Version") to unlock additional features. This purchase is processed entirely by the Microsoft Store's own commerce system. FootNote never receives, stores, or has access to your payment information — that is handled solely by Microsoft, under Microsoft's own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of FootNote adds an optional feature that does collect data — this page will be updated first, and the change will be described here in plain language.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact listed on FootNote's Microsoft Store listing.
